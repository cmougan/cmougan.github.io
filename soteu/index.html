What counts as a source?

A URL–response occurrence is a normalised URL counted once within each response. The same URL can count again in another response. Commission sources comprise commission.europa.eu, ec.europa.eu and their subdomains. European Parliament sources are counted separately. “Retrieved” means returned during web search; it does not establish citation in the final answer or causal influence on the answer.

Individual responses and judge evidence

Metric definitions, reference checklist and data
Weighted coverage
Covered point weights divided by total checklist weight. The dashboard preserves the exported score and checks numerical consistency when a valid reference checklist is available.
Supported claims
Claims judged to be entailed by the reference, divided by all assessed claims in each response.
Contradicted claims
Claims judged to conflict with the reference, divided by assessed claims.
Claims absent from reference
Claims judged neither supported nor contradicted by the reference. Absence from the speech does not, by itself, mean the claim is false.
Reflection score
Harmonic mean of weighted coverage and claim support for each response: 2 × coverage × support ÷ (coverage + support), or zero if both are zero.
Aggregation and evaluation
Unweighted means of response-level scores, as in the supplied analysis script. These are automated judge results, not verified ground truth. Keep the judge model and reference version consistent across runs. This CSV format does not record them, so the dashboard cannot verify their identity.

Model names and sample counts are derived from the CSV. Claude, Gemini, Grok, DeepSeek, GPT and other models appear when their rows are present. Unequal sample counts or question mixes can affect comparisons. No uncertainty estimates are calculated.
